## 👨‍🔬 1. MAXWELL "MAX" THORNTON (The Unhinged Genius)

```text
==================================================================================================
ATTRIBUTE                SPECIFICATION
==================================================================================================
Age                      28
Role                     Protagonist / Experimental Inventor & Theoretical Physicist
Visual Design            Wild unkempt brown hair, copper-rimmed singed safety goggles, lab coat over vintage band tee, always carrying a modified torque wrench.
Personality              Hyperactive, optimistic to a fault, oblivious to danger, speaks in rapid-fire technical jargon.
Motivations              To invent mundane household appliances powered by quantum mechanics.
Catchphrase              "It’s not broken, it’s just operating in four dimensions simultaneously!"
Comedic Flaw             Every invention he builds to "save time" ends up taking 3 weeks and half a neighborhood block to repair.
==================================================================================================
```

## ☕ 2. LEO VANCE (The Deadpan Pragmatist)

```text
==================================================================================================
ATTRIBUTE                SPECIFICATION
==================================================================================================
Age                      29
Role                     Co-Protagonist / Freelance Graphic Designer & Straight Man
Visual Design            Sleek black hair, silk bathrobe or oversized hoodie, permanent dark circles under eyes, holding an espresso cup.
Personality              Deadpan, sarcastic, hyper-observant, cool under pressure, immune to chaos.
Motivations              To drink his coffee in peace and avoid getting evicted.
Catchphrase              "Max, I’m giving you five seconds to un-invent whatever is currently vibrating."
Comedic Flaw             Refuses to leave the couch even when the living room is experiencing zero-gravity.
==================================================================================================
```

## 📋 3. MR. BALDEV GUPTA (The Explosive Landlord)

```text
==================================================================================================
ATTRIBUTE                SPECIFICATION
==================================================================================================
Age                      63
Role                     Primary Antagonist / Apartment Building Owner & Rent Collector
Visual Design            Short, stout, wearing a checkered cardigan, holding a clipboard, toupee that shifts position depending on rage levels.
Personality              Hot-tempered, money-obsessed, suspicious of everything, surprisingly agile when chasing tenants.
Motivations              To collect rent on the 1st of the month and enforce the "No Pyrotechnics" lease clause.
Catchphrase              "If my drywall is charred again, your deposit is atomized!"
Comedic Flaw             Can be immediately appeased if offered exotic baked goods or high-tech plumbing upgrades.
==================================================================================================
```
