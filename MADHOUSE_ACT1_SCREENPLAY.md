# 🎭 MAD HOUSE: ACT 1 SCREENPLAY (SCENE 1 & 2)
*Genre: High-Octane Comedy / Animated Series & Comic Book Master Script*

---

## 🎬 SCENE 1: EXT. THE MAD HOUSE - MORNING

```text
[CAMERA] Pan down from a bright neon sign that flickers: "THE MAD HOUSE — APARTMENTS FOR THE UNHINGED".
A pigeon lands on the sign. A sudden small explosion from Apartment 4B knocks the pigeon completely out of frame.

SFX: BOOM! (followed by sizzle and faint disco music)
```

**INT. APARTMENT 4B - LIVING ROOM - CONTINUOUS**

SMOKE fills the room. 

**MAX (28)** — hyperactive inventor, wearing singed safety goggles and an oven mitt — stands over a smoking toaster that is levitating three inches off the counter.

Max coughs, wiping soot from his cheek.

**MAX**  
(triumphant)  
I’ve done it. The quantum butter-spreader is operational!

**LEO (29)** — cool, sarcastic roommate, lounging in a bathrobe while sipping espresso — doesn't even look up from his phone.

**LEO**  
Max. It’s 8:05 AM. The landlord is coming in ten minutes to inspect the "no-pyrotechnics" clause.

**MAX**  
It’s not pyrotechnics! It’s sub-atomic toast optimization! Watch.

Max presses a red button on the toaster. A slice of sourdough shoots out at Mach 2, embeds itself into the wall, and leaves a smoking crater.

**LEO**  
(pauses, evaluates crater)  
Impressive velocity. Still unbuttered, though.

```text
SFX: HEAVY KNOCKING AT THE FRONT DOOR (BAM! BAM! BAM!)
```

**MR. GUPTA (O.S.)**  
(screaming through keyhole)  
MAX! LEO! I smelt smoke! If my drywall is charred again, I’m raising your rent by four hundred percent!

---

## 🎬 SCENE 2: INT. APARTMENT 4B - HALLWAY / DOOR - CONTINUOUS

Leo calmly walks over to the door, placing a fake plastic potted plant over the smoking wall crater.

**LEO**  
(whispering to Max)  
Hide the toaster. Act normal.

Max frantically shoves the quantum toaster inside a refrigerator. The refrigerator immediately starts humming in D-minor and emitting purple light.

Leo opens the door with a wide, charming smile.

**MR. GUPTA (60s)** — furious landlord, holding a clipboard and wearing an ill-fitting toupee — stomps inside.

**MR. GUPTA**  
What was that noise?! It sounded like a surface-to-air missile!

**LEO**  
Missile? Mr. Gupta, please. That was merely Max’s passion for gourmet baking. We’re testing high-altitude yeast.

Mr. Gupta eyes the room suspiciously. He walks toward the potted plant.

**MR. GUPTA**  
Why is this fern smoking?

**MAX**  
(sweating heavily)  
It’s... a botanical humidifier! Very popular in Tokyo!

Suddenly, the refrigerator doors BURST OPEN. A toasted bagel floats into the air, surrounded by a glowing blue forcefield.

**MR. GUPTA**  
(gasping, toupee lifts slightly)  
WHAT IN THE NAME OF RENT CONTROL IS THAT?!

**LEO**  
(without missing a beat)  
That, Mr. Gupta, is breakfast. Would you like a bite?
